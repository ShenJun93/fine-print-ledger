    What is trusted here. Only an entity's $creator is trusted: the chain sets it at creation and nobody can change it. Every other field (event, domain, urlHash, version, even contentHash) can be written by anyone with a wallet. So this app queries with createdBy(snapshotter) and labels every other creator as untrusted. Snapshots are created readonly, so not even the snapshotter can edit one after the fact.
